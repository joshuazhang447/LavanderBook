# LavenderBook privacy policy

> The policy, written from what LavenderBook actually does. Shown in the app for now, at
> `https://lavenderbook.oau.app/account/privacy`, and meant to move to the main site. Edit it
> here, then run `node scripts/build-privacy-policy.mjs`: the app's copy is generated from this.
> Have someone who knows Canadian privacy law read it.

_Last updated: November 14, 2026_

LavenderBook is a map and directory of LGBTQ+ places - community centres, clinics, shelters,
support groups, queer-owned businesses - with reviews from the people who visit them. It is
made by One Among Us.

This page says what LavenderBook collects, who can see it, and what you can do about it.

- You can use the map, the directory and every review without an account.
- Your location is optional, and your exact position never leaves your device.
- If you sign in, you sign in with Google, and nobody else ever sees your Google name or
  email. Other people see a made-up name, and by default a different one at every place you
  review.
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share your information for
  marketing.

## Who we are

LavenderBook is run by One Among Us, a community organisation in Toronto. Questions,
requests and complaints about your information go to Estelle, LavenderBook's Lead Developer,
at **estelle@oneamongus.ca**.

## Using LavenderBook without an account

You can read the map, the directory and everyone's reviews without signing in. Nothing you
look at is linked to you or to an account, and we keep no record of what you search for or
which places you open. Like any website, our server's technical logs briefly record
requests and the internet address they came from, to keep it running.

## Signing in

You need an account only to post reviews, answer a place's questions, or report a review.
Accounts are created by signing in with Google. There is no password with us.

**What we receive from Google:** your Google account ID, your email address, your name and
your profile picture, as Google sends them. We use them only to sign you in and to tell your
account apart from others. They are never shown to anyone in the app, including the people
who moderate it.

**Your public name:** when you sign in for the first time, LavenderBook gives your account a
random name. It has nothing to do with your Google name.

## What other people can see

When you post a review, everyone can read:

- your stars, your words, and your answers to the place's questions;
- **a name**: by default each review gets a name of its own, different at every place, so
  your reviews cannot be lined up by name. You can turn this off in your account settings;
  your account's random name is then used instead for later reviews;
- **roughly when you posted** ("a few weeks ago"), counted in whole weeks. If you choose to
  show dates, readers see the day instead. Nobody else ever sees the exact time.

Reviews go through a word filter. A review using certain words waits for a moderator before
anyone else can see it; you can still see it yourself. If someone reports a review, the
author is not told who reported it.

## Your location

Location is optional. You choose one of three:

- **Near me**: the map follows your device. Only this choice asks your device for permission.
- **General area**: worked out from your device's time zone. Nothing is sent to do it.
- **A city you pick.**

Your choice is stored on your device only, never on your account.

**Your exact position never leaves your device.** To load the places around you, the app
sends the centre of the 250-metre square you are in. To help a search, it sends your
position rounded to about 11 km. Both are sent without your account attached, so they cannot
be linked to you. Distances are worked out on your device.

We never look up where you are from your internet address, and nothing in the app tries to
tell which country you are in.

## What is stored on your device

The app remembers, on your device only:

- that you have read the safety notice;
- your location choice, the look you picked, and the search distance;
- if you are signed in, the sign-in session.

**Leave now**, on the safety notice and on the website, erases all of this at once, ends your
session, and takes you to an everyday website.

## What we store on our server

For each account:

- your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture, from Google;
- your random account name, and your settings (names per place, showing dates);
- your reviews and answers, and the names and dates shown with them;
- the reviews you report, and why;
- places you add by reviewing them: their name and position, which are public.

To keep the service working and safe, we also keep the word filter's results, the
moderators' decisions, and whether an account has been banned.

The database runs on a server One Among Us rents from Contabo, a hosting company, in a data
centre in Canada. Cloudflare sits in front of it and passes traffic through. The database is backed up every night, and each
backup is deleted after 14 days.

## Who can see what, inside LavenderBook

**Moderators** are a small group of people at One Among Us. In the admin panel they can see
every review (including ones waiting for a decision), the exact time each was posted, which
account wrote it, its account name next to its review name, and who reported what. They can
publish, hide, edit or remove reviews, and ban accounts. The admin panel does not show your
email address or your Google name.

The people who run the server can technically reach everything stored on it. They only do so
to keep the service running, or when the law requires it.

## Services we rely on

When your device talks to these services directly, they see your internet address, as any
website would:

- **Google**: sign-in; the map on Android and on the website. Place searches go through our
  server, so Google sees our server, not your device, with the search words and the rounded
  position.
- **Apple Maps**: the map on iPhone.
- **Cloudflare**: delivers the website and our server's traffic.
- **Expo**: checks for app updates when the app opens.

Each of these has its own privacy policy. We send none of them your account, your reviews or
your location beyond what is described above.

## What we do not do

- No ads, and no advertising or analytics code in the app.
- No selling, renting or trading your information.
- No location history, and no tracking across apps or websites.

## Your choices and rights

- **Delete a review**: from your account page, at any time.
- **Change how you appear**: names per place and showing dates are in your account settings.
- **See, correct or delete everything about you**: email **estelle@oneamongus.ca** from the
  address you sign in with. Deleting your account removes your reviews and answers too, and
  backups containing it are gone within 14 days. We answer within 30 days. A button to delete
  your account in the app itself is on the way.
- **Complaints**: write to us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy
  Commissioner of Canada. In Ontario, the federal privacy law (PIPEDA) covers
  organisations like ours.

## Age

LavenderBook is for everyone, and there is no minimum age to use the map, the directory or
the reviews: they list places and what visitors said about them. Posting needs a Google
account, so Google's own age rules apply to signing in.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new date. If a change affects
what we collect or who sees it, we will say so in the app before it takes effect.
